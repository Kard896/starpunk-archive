---

### Overview

Valoris, often called **“The Crown,”** stands as the luminous heart of the Polaris Coalition — a planet built on the ideals of courage, unity, and civilization’s eternal flame. From orbit, its continents shimmer in geometric lattices of gold and white, a testament to centuries of refinement and meticulous control. Every surface, every city, was once a deliberate act of faith in order — faith that the Coalition’s light would never fade.

The seat of **His Majesty’s Throne** remains in **Aeternum**, a spired megacity of marble skies and mirrored causeways. Here, the Senate of Stars once convened beneath the crystal dome known as the *Celestial Crown*, where leaders of the Coalition debated policy, poetry, and the future of humankind with equal reverence.

---

### Geography & Structure

Valoris is an engineered world, restructured long ago through careful geoforming. The planet’s three primary regions — **The Crownlands**, **Eldhaven Reach**, and **The Saint’s Belt** — represent the Coalition’s layered ideals:

- **The Crownlands**  
  The political and cultural core of the planet. Its gleaming towers are sustained by orbital mirrors that simulate an eternal dawn, symbolizing vigilance and purity. Most of the nobility reside here, insulated within marble districts of diplomatic grace and quiet unrest.

- **Eldhaven Reach**  
  A sprawling expanse of reclaimed wilderness once dedicated to preservation and education. Today, its universities and sanctuaries serve as the Coalition’s living memory — places where young scholars are taught to “guard the light of history” from fading. The name honors *Eldhaven*, the Coalition world of refuge and reflection.

- **The Saint’s Belt**  
  A crescent of orbital settlements named in reverence to *Saint Corval*, patron of courage and the lost. The Belt’s stations house millions, maintaining Valoris’s agricultural and resource equilibrium. Pilgrims from across the stars once journeyed here seeking strength and renewal.

---

### Society & Governance

Valoris was conceived as the physical embodiment of the Coalition’s highest ideals. Citizenship is stratified not by birthright but by **service to the Crown**, a tradition that once bound every world to its shared oath. From the **Order of Luminous Engineers** to the **Chamber of Silence**, every guild and office serves a singular mandate — to preserve the image of perfection the Coalition projects to the galaxy.

Ceremony dominates daily life. From the royal guards' radiant plate to the banners that unfurl each morning with the rising mirrors, the Crown’s citizens live within a performance of legacy. Even decay is hidden beneath gold.

---

### Legacy

Though centuries of change have eroded the Coalition’s unity, **Valoris endures** — immaculate, self-aware, and quietly haunted by its own perfection. The planet remains both a beacon and a tomb; its beauty, an artifact; its silence, a sermon.

Visitors describe the experience of standing in the Hall of Light as both humbling and hollow — *“as if the air itself remembers what courage once felt like.”*

---

### Notes of Interest

- The **Crown Archives** hold every recorded decree since the Founding Concord. Access is restricted to high-ranking officials and archivists of the Seventh Seal.  
- The **Saint Corval Reliquary** within the Belt is the final resting site of countless unnamed guardians whose deeds are inscribed only in light.  
- **Eldhaven’s Academy of Memory** maintains sister institutions on Valoris, ensuring continuity of thought even as the Coalition wanes.
