*"Progress once came to Deralo on silver wings; it left on faster ones."* — Archivist’s Note, Crown Historical Survey

---

### Overview

Deralo was once regarded as the **industrial heart** of the Outer Crown, a frontier world rich in volatile minerals, plasma wells, and crystal veins that powered the expansion of the **Polaris Coalition** during its height. Its people were pragmatic, fiercely proud, and deeply loyal to the Crown that lifted them from dust to prosperity.

Today, Deralo stands as both a relic and a lesson — a world that rose swiftly, burned brightly, and was left adrift in the wake of technological evolution.

---

### Early History

When the **Coalition reached beyond Valoris**, Deralo was among the first independent systems to petition for membership. Its leaders saw unity not as submission, but as opportunity. Within a generation, the planet’s industries were fully integrated into the Coalition’s supply network, producing vast quantities of alloy, antimatter components, and hull composites for interstellar construction.

The planet’s surface bore the marks of ambition: sprawling foundries, floating freight towers, and dense worker colonies arranged around the equatorial belt. Coalition engineers hailed Deralo as a model of “productive harmony,” where resource extraction and civic expansion moved hand in hand.

---

### The Age of Expansion

The boom years of Deralo are defined by two names — **Ephyra** and **Olympus**.

- **Ephyra**  
  The planet’s largest spaceport and economic core. Built within a cratered basin at the edge of the Sapphire Wastes, Ephyra grew into a megacity of perpetual twilight — a nexus of trade where cargo from across the Crown was processed, refined, and dispatched along the old hyperlanes.

- **Olympus**  
  Suspended above Ephyra’s skyline, Olympus served as Deralo’s orbital capital — an airborne sanctuary of glass and gold. Reserved for dignitaries, executives, and visiting Coalition envoys, Olympus symbolized Deralo’s ascension from a frontier world to a jewel of civilization. To see its lights from the ground was to glimpse heaven itself.

During this period, Deralo’s exports rivaled those of entire sectors. Eldhaven’s academies wrote admiring essays on Deralo’s “industrial renaissance,” and Valoris proclaimed it a **“World of Exemplary Contribution.”**

---

### Decline and Disconnection

Deralo’s fall was not born of rebellion, but of obsolescence.  
When the Coalition adopted the **Second-Generation Hyperlane Network**, older trade corridors — including the Valoris–Deralo route — were bypassed. The new routes favored systems with cleaner gravity wells and easier access to the Core Gates. Deralo’s prosperity, once dependent on constant traffic, began to wither.

By the time the Crown Senate voted to retire the *Ephyra Exchange Charter*, Deralo’s skies had dimmed. Olympus, once radiant, became a hollowed luxury city, its plazas echoing with silence as dignitaries withdrew and maintenance drones took their place.

Many citizens emigrated to Saint Corval’s sanctuaries or Eldhaven’s open colonies. Those who remained adapted, reclaiming old foundries as habitation domes and trading memories of glory for survival among the stars.

---

### Legacy

In the modern era, Deralo endures as a haunting monument to the Coalition’s reach — a place where ambition touched the divine and then fell back to dust.  
Its cities, though decayed, still function. Its orbital lines still glimmer faintly at night. And from the surface, Olympus can still be seen drifting overhead, **a ghost of privilege** slowly circling the world that built it.

Scholars from Valoris call Deralo *“a wound that learned to heal without forgetting the cut.”*  
Visitors simply call it **home.**
