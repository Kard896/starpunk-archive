# Overview

Deralo is a post-collapse world of wind-scoured basins, fossil megacities, and stubborn communities that learned to survive between dust storms and Grok zones. It’s where **Neeks** first learned what was worth saving—and where **Luna** later returns to sift through the Polaris Coalition’s ghost tech.

> **At a glance**
> - **Type:** Arid terrestrial, thin atmosphere pockets, heavy ion storms  
> - **Era:** Post-Coalition collapse, “Second Spark” age  
> - **Notables:** Grok outbreaks, Weex residue, QT-reactive relics  
> - **Themes:** Identity, adaptation, memory, repair

## Geography & Climate

- **Salt Flats of Vareen:** Blinding plains hiding sub-surface conduits once used for Coalition freight. Ion storms roll in fast; visibility can drop to meters.
- **Riftline Corridors:** Canyon networks that channel seasonal winds and serve as migration paths for both caravans and Grok swarms.
- **Deralo Prime (Old Metro):** A stacked ruin of transit rings and collapsed skydocks. Scavengers call it “the Chime” for the way torn girders sing in crosswinds.

## History (Quick Timeline)

- **Pre-Coalition:** Sparse colonization; terraforming attempts stall.  
- **Coalition Build-Up:** Deralo becomes a logistics hub for Atom’s Polaris Coalition.  
- **The Fall:** Coalition implodes; Grok events spike; memory-reactive tech is sealed or scrapped.  
- **Second Spark:** Small polities and caravans stabilize routes; Luna’s era of rediscovery begins.

## The Weex & Residual Tech

**Weex** (memory-consuming shapeshifters) once threaded their designs through Deralo. Even now, **QT-class interfaces** react to buried latticework, caches, and failsafe doors keyed to *intent and recall*.

- **Wards & Fail-safes:** Coalition relics that only open under specific cognitive “signatures.”  
- **Echo Nodes:** Nodes that imprint brief sensory or mnemonic fragments—useful but risky for Grok attraction.

## Groks (Threat Profile)

**Groks** are what happens when **Weex remains** and stray memory-mass stew together too long. On Deralo they form quickly after dust-ups, clustering near metal-rich wreckage.

- **Behavior:** Drawn to recent thought-imprints and kinetic noise.
- **Countermeasures:** Silence, low-heat traversal, and throwing off decoys; caravans carry “dumb beacons” to bleed attention.

## Factions & People

- **Ringjack Caravans:** Nomads who run the riftlines; trade in maps, clean water, and Grok-quieted routes.
- **Spindle Guilds:** Tech-tinkerers who refurbish Coalition parts into tools and shelter systems.
- **Cartel “Protection” (Sebastian era):** Offers real safety at a cost—especially around metro approaches.

## Why Deralo Matters to the Story

- **Neeks’ Past:** Early failures and first contact with QT-class logic shape her identity arc.
- **Luna’s Present:** Deralo’s relic network *recognizes* Luna—doors open, secrets spill, and dangers follow.
- **Central Theme:** The planet literalizes memory: what you carry defines what wakes up around you.

## Key Locations

- **The Chime (Deralo Prime):** Transit rings, vault nodes, and Grok “shadow pools.”  
- **Oxbow Array:** A half-buried satellite farm still whispering to anything that listens.  
- **Rook’s Ladder:** A canyon lift where caravans refuel and swap storm intel.

## Traveler Notes (In-World)

- Pack **ion shrouds** and **sound baffles**.  
- Never cross salt flats after dusk without a line to the **ring beacons**.  
- If a vault **asks your name**, answer with one you can afford to lose.
