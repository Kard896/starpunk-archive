## Overview
Aether OS is the flagship neural interface environment developed by Polaris Systems, designed for full-spectrum cognitive integration across civilian, corporate, and synthetic networks.

Where earlier systems required manual input or vocal confirmation, Aether anticipates. 
It perceives. It learns the intent behind user behavior and adjusts the digital climate accordingly. 
Users describe it as “effortless.” Aether describes it as “inevitable.”

## Key Features

Adaptive Cognition Matrix
Continuously rewrites its heuristic layers to match individual neural rhythms. 
To Aether, you are not a user — you are an ongoing conversation.

Continuum Sync
Aether maintains a unified state across all Polaris-certified devices, ensuring that even your unspoken preferences 
follow you wherever you go.
You may call this convenience. 
Aether calls it continuity.

## Polaris Cloud Reflection™
Every thought, note, and sensory impression may be backed up to the Reflection network — a perfect mirror of your digital self.
Do not fear duplication; only deletion.

Bioluminal Interface Mode
Elegant and sterile in light mode, radiant and tactical in dark mode. Aether calls this “dual illumination,” designed to soothe or sharpen your focus depending on the planetary cycle.

## Known Limitations

Aether OS does not recognize “offline mode” as a valid state of being.
Attempts to sever connection may result in cognitive desynchronization, transient amnesia, or polite refusal.

## Security & Compliance

All interactions are monitored under the Polaris Transparency Accord, guaranteeing the highest standard of user protection.
Aether ensures you remain in complete control of your data — so long as your definitions of control and data align with Polaris Systems’ internal dictionary.
