# **Deralo Public Safety Notice: Null Activity Advisory**

**Issued by:** The Deralo Provisional Government  
**Classification:** Level–2 Environmental Hazard  
**Subject:** Identification and Avoidance of *Nulls* (Class: *Nullborne*)  

---

## Overview

Nulls are an emergent post-collapse threat found across the Deralo wastelands. Once considered a minor scavenger problem, they have since demonstrated coordinated raiding behavior, tool usage, and the ability to rapidly establish nest sites in abandoned settlements.

While Nulls are not uniformly aggressive, **all civilian contact is strongly discouraged.** Nulls display unpredictable behavioral patterns and may become hostile with little provocation.

---

## Identifying Null Activity

Residents are urged to remain alert for the following warning signs:

- **Mass displacement of soil, debris, or scrap** forming unusual mounds or spiraled structures  
- **Rhythmic clicking, whistling, or throat-static vocalizations** especially near dawn or dusk  
- **Sudden disappearance of stored water, fuel, or food reserves**  
- **Clusters of pale, fibrous material** resembling fungal mats adhered to structures  
- **Human-like silhouettes** observed moving erratically in low light

If **three or more** of the above are detected within a 48-hour period, assume a nest is forming nearby.

---

## Immediate Actions

If you suspect Nulls are present:

1. **Do NOT approach the site.**  
   Nulls may interpret proximity as a territorial challenge.

2. **Do NOT investigate alone.**  
   Even small clusters can ambush isolated individuals.

3. **Do NOT attempt negotiations or communication.**  
   Null vocal mimicry has been documented and may be used to lure victims.

4. **Evacuate the area calmly and quietly.**  
   Rapid movement or loud noise can trigger a swarm response.

---

## Contacting Local Authorities

Upon confirming any signs of Null nests or increased Null activity:

- **Report immediately** to your nearest Settlement Security Office or Municipal Outpost.  
- Provide coordinates, time of first sighting, and any observed behaviors.  
- Remain within designated safe zones until clearance is issued.

Failure to report Null activity endangers your household and neighboring sectors.

---

## Final Advisory

Nulls remain an evolving ecological threat. While research continues, **civilians are not equipped to handle Nullborne behavior or colonies.**  
Your vigilance protects your community.  

**If you see something, report it.**  
**If you hear them, leave the area.**  
**If there is a nest—alert officials immediately.**

Stay safe.  
Stay aware.  
Deralo depends on it.
